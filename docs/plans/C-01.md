# C-01 — Lead capture and duplicate detection

## Goal

One `LeadIntakeService` that every source uses (website form, WhatsApp, agent referral, email, staff entry, phone call). It records where
the lead came from (source, UTM, referrer, agent code), finds an existing patient by phone, email or passport (a similar name is only a
hint), and creates the case in stage `enquiry`. Staff see the leads in an inbox, with a "possible duplicate" banner and a merge action.
Acceptance: the same phone number submitted twice creates one patient with two enquiries, both flagged.

## Design

- **Data** (migration `c_01_leads`): `Lead` (source, UTM source/medium/campaign, referrer, agent code, language, raw message, the
  name/phone/email/passport as submitted, `matchedOn`, `duplicateState` none | possible | dismissed | merged, `duplicateOfPatientId`,
  case and patient, who entered it); `Patient.mergedIntoId/mergedAt`; expression indexes on the digits of `Patient.phone` and the
  letters-and-digits of `Patient.passportNumber`, so matching ignores spaces, dashes and case without a new column to keep in step.
- **Matching** (`LeadIntakeService.findMatches`): phone (digits only), email (lower case), passport (upper-case alphanumerics) are
  *strong*: the lead is attached to that patient (no second patient) and the new lead, and that patient's earlier open leads, are flagged
  `possible`. A name with a bigram similarity of at least 0.8 (token order ignored, accents stripped) is a *hint*: a new patient is
  created, the lead is flagged with the candidate, and staff may merge. No database extension is needed.
- **Creating the case**: `CasesService.openEnquiry(tx, …)` is the old body of `create`, taken out so staff-created cases and leads use
  one code path (number, first-response due, SLA, stage history, auto-tasks, audit, outbox, realtime). Leads have no owner yet
  (assignment is C-02); a staff member who enters a lead owns it if they are a case manager.
- **Public form** `POST /public/leads` (no sign-in): Cloudflare Turnstile token, a honeypot field, 5 per hour per address, Zod body.
  The answer is only the enquiry reference: it never says whether the person was already known. `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` is required in
  production; in development and tests an unset key accepts any token (and says so in the log).
- **Staff API** (`Case` permission, scoped like cases; agents, patients, hospitals have none of it): `POST /leads` (manual entry),
  `GET /leads` (cursor, filters: source, duplicate state, search), `GET /leads/:id`, `POST /leads/:id/dismiss-duplicate`,
  `POST /leads/:id/merge` (merge the lead's patient into the candidate). Merge moves every patient-owned row (cases, documents,
  consents, attendants, visa applications, feedback) and the other leads, keeps the portal login if only one patient has one, refuses
  when both have one, soft-deletes the merged patient with `mergedIntoId`, and audits `patient.merged` in the same transaction.
- **Audit and logs**: `lead.created` and `patient.merged` hold ids and field names, never values; request logs already redact PII.
- **Portal** `/ops/leads`: the inbox (filters, search, duplicate badge), a new-lead dialog, and a detail dialog with the banner,
  matched fields, the other enquiries of the same patient, "Merge into the other patient", "Not a duplicate" and "Open case".

## Assumptions

Agent referral leads (`source: agent`) name the agency by referral code and are linked to it. WhatsApp and email listeners are
other tasks: they will call `LeadIntakeService.intake`. Passport numbers are only captured by staff entry for now. Raw messages may
hold medical text, so the lead is visible only to case staff, and it is not copied anywhere else.
