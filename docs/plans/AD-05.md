# AD-05 — Pricing rules and FX

## Goal

The super admin sets what we charge: the Basic, Standard and Premium service packages and the add-on price list (extra night,
interpreter hour, SIM, sightseeing, wheelchair transfer), margins per quote component, discount limits per role, and the exchange-rate
settings (provider, markup, manual rate with a reason). Every price change is a new version and quotes already made keep the price they
were made with. Acceptance: changing the Premium price does not alter quotes already sent.

## Design

- **Data** (migrations `ad_05_pricing`, `ad_05_checks`): `PriceItem` (kind `service_package` or `add_on`, unique code, name, unit,
  inclusions, active, sort order); `PriceItemVersion` (item, version number, note, who) with `PriceItemPrice` rows (currency, minor
  units); `MarginRule` (one row per quote line kind, basis points); `DiscountThreshold` (one row per role, basis points);
  `FxSetting` (single row `default`: provider or null for the environment's, markup in basis points); `FxOverride` (pair, rate, reason,
  optional expiry, revoked time). Check constraints: no negative amount, positive rate, a pair of two currencies, one settings row.
- **Versions are immutable.** Publishing writes version N+1 holding the full set of prices (under an advisory lock per item, so two
  admins cannot both become version 4). The current price is the newest version. A quote line copies the price (and, through the lookup,
  the version and the FX snapshot) when it is made, and never reads the price list again, so a later version cannot change it. The
  quote builder (C-xx) calls `GET /admin/pricing/items/:code/price?currency=` and stores what it returns.
- **Auto-conversion.** The INR price is required. A currency without its own price is the INR price converted with `convert()` from
  `@medtravel/fx` (the stored rate, or a manual one) plus the markup, rounded half-even.
- **Manual rates.** `rateOn()` looks at `FxOverride` before the stored rates: direct, then inverse. An override is in force from its
  creation until it expires or is revoked, and a lookup for a past day sees the override that was in force then. A new override for a
  pair revokes the one before it.
- **Provider setting.** The worker's daily refresh reads `FxSetting.provider` at run time (null means `FX_PROVIDER`); the markup is read
  by the price lookup. `openexchangerates` still needs `OPEN_EXCHANGE_RATES_APP_ID` in the environment.
- **Margins and discounts.** Stored and audited here; applied by the quote builder. `applyMargin`, `discountBp` and `discountDecision`
  are pure functions in `@medtravel/contracts` (unit tested) so the builder and the approval step use the same rules.
- **API** (`/admin/pricing`, subject `Setting`, super admin only, except where noted): `GET/POST items`, `PUT items/:code`,
  `POST items/:code/prices`, `GET items/:code/history`, `GET items/:code/price` (`create Quote`: case manager, super admin),
  `GET/PUT margins`, `GET discounts` (`create Quote`) and `PUT discounts`, `GET/PUT fx`, `GET/POST fx/overrides`,
  `DELETE fx/overrides/:id`. Every write is audited (`pricing.*`); publishing and setting a manual rate also queue an outbox event.
- **Portal** `/admin/pricing`: three tabs (price list with set prices, history and edit dialogs; margins and discount limits; rate
  settings and manual rates).

## Assumptions

Prices have no translations yet (names and inclusions are English text the admin types). Package prices are per package, not per
hospital or treatment. Margins apply to cost, in whole basis points. Discount limits must not fall as the role goes up (case manager
≤ finance ≤ super admin). Finance can see the pricing menu but the API refuses them: they have no `Setting` permission.
