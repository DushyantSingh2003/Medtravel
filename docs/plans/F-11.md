# F-11 — Seed data and test factories

## Goal

After one command every portal shows believable data, and tests can build rows quickly.

## Design

- `packages/db/prisma/seed-data/demo.ts`, run by `seed.ts` when `SEED_DEMO_DATA=true`: three fictional hospitals (Delhi, Mumbai,
  Chennai) with departments, 15 doctors and 30 packages; three agencies (Iraq, Uzbekistan, Kenya); 40 patients with cases spread across
  all 12 stages plus a closed case, an on-hold case and five lost cases at different points. Each case carries what its stage would
  have: intake, history, summary and documents (metadata), hospital opinion requests with consent and answered opinions, quote and
  lines, deposit and service payments, visa application, bookings, tasks, a conversation with messages, and agency stage-sharing consent.
- Idempotent: hospitals, doctors, packages and agencies are upserted by stable keys; the patients and everything under them are
  inserted once in a single transaction, guarded by the first demo case number (demo cases are numbered 900001 onwards).
- The development users are wired in: the hospital desk and doctor belong to the Delhi hospital (the doctor to its first doctor), the
  agent to the Iraqi agency, the ground coordinator to Delhi, and the patient user owns the first case.
- `pnpm db:reset` (development only): `prisma migrate reset --force`, then the seed with dev users and demo data.
- Factories: `@medtravel/db/testing` (`createFactories(prisma)`): users, patients, hospitals, doctors, agents, cases at any stage, quotes,
  payments, tasks, with counters for unique values and overrides.

## Assumptions

Documents are metadata only (no file in the bucket), so opening one fails until real files are uploaded; the names, phones and
emails are invented (`@demo.medtravel.test`).
